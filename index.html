Frequently asked questions

Is Athlomate free?

You can sign up and start finding players and games for free.

Where is it available?

We are launching in Greece first, with the app available in English and Greek.

Which sports are supported?

We launch with the most popular sports in Greece, such as football, basketball, tennis and padel, volleyball, running, swimming, cycling, yoga, pilates and calisthenics.

Do you share my exact location?

No. Athlomate only uses your location once to suggest your city. Only the city you choose is saved to your profile.
